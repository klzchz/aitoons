Privacy Policy

When the AIToons TikTok account is connected, the tool stores the TikTok open ID, display name, avatar URL and access tokens, used only to publish videos the owner chooses and to show the post status.

We do not sell, share or use this data for any other purpose. Disconnecting deletes the tokens. Contact: aitoons team via the TikTok account @watchaitoons.
